How Snapduo works

Snapduo is crafted for intimate, ephemeral photobooth moments between two friends anywhere across the globe. Simple, private, and nostalgic by design.

sensors

Direct connection

Your video stream routes straight to your friend's screen without lingering on our servers.

Under the hood

Direct peer-to-peer piping keeps live calls strictly between the two of you.

photo_camera

Photos on your phone

Each phone triggers its own camera hardware shutter, then pieces the tandem strip together.

Crisp prints

Full sensor clarity instead of fuzzy screen captures from video compression.

hourglass_empty

Gone in 24 hours

Shared links and photo strips automatically vanish from the web after twenty-four hours.

Zero footprint

No profile databases, no persistent storage, and no tracking cookies.

STRIP-04 // MEMORY ARTIFACT

A timeless physical keepsake, digital first

Snap four dynamic frames in synchronized sequence. Print, save to your camera roll, or send the ephemeral strip to friends before it dissolves.
